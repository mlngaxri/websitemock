Initial Direction

01 / Initial directionSubmitted 18 Sep

Japanese restaurant · Brisbane

Mori House

We need a site that makes the food and the room feel like the same place. People should find the menu, hours and reservation details quickly.

M
01 / WHAT MATTERS

Help guests plan dinner

Our menu changes with the season, but guests still need a clear idea of what we serve and what dinner costs. Keep the hours and reservation information easy to find from every page.

Main actionReserve a table
02 / THE RESTAURANT

28 seats in Brisbane

We serve dinner Tuesday to Saturday. The room is small, with timber, warm light and an open view of the kitchen. Reservations open four weeks ahead; guests can note dietary needs when booking.

03 / SITE CONTENT

Home, Menu, Visit

Home Introduce the room and the cookingMenu Show current dishes and pricesVisit Hours, location and reservations
04 / VISUAL REFERENCE

Let the photography lead

Please use the room photographs for the opening and Visit pages. The close-up food images belong on Menu. Keep the layout quiet and the dish names easy to read.

05 / FILES PROVIDED

Images for the site

Room photography, kitchen photography and individual dishes supplied with the initial direction.

WEBSITE APPROVAL PENDING

The site is ready for review. Add any changes in Review, then approve the website when the final version is right.
